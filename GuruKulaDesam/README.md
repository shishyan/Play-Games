# Guru Kula Desam (குரு குல தேசம்)

**Play Live on Roblox:** [https://www.roblox.com/games/118864259076942](https://www.roblox.com/games/118864259076942)

## Overview
**Guru Kula Desam** is a WGT-style **image-driven 3D pilgrimage, virtual Gurukulam school, and Samsara Karma RPG** spanning the entire map of India (`Bharatavarsha`) and Sri Lanka with folded geographic distance.

### Core Pillars
1. **Brahma's Law of Samsara, Karma & Reincarnation (`TempleConfig.SamsaraIncarnations`)**:
   - Every player begins at **0 Good Karma** in a humble Seva incarnation (`Tier 1: Shudra / Seva Pilgrim — 5 Active Doshas`).
   - Studying at the **Virtual Gurukulam School**, climbing **Sacred Temple Hill Steps (`Padigal`)**, performing **Garbhagriha Darshan**, and doing **Annadanam / Cow Seva** earns **Good Karma (`Punya`)**.
   - Performing bad/adharmic actions (`Tamas` laziness, `Krodha` harsh speech, or polluting sacred hills) **erases Good Karma (`Papa`)**.
   - As your Karma grows, your soul reincarnates into higher births with superior **Life Quality**, higher walk speed, unlocked vehicles, and **fewer Doshas (sufferings)** — ascending through **7 Reincarnation Tiers** up to **Brahma-Tejas Jivanmukta (`2,500+ Karma, 0 Doshas`)**.
2. **12 Regional Virtual Gurukulam School Zones (`Grades 1 to 12`)**:
   - Mapped across 12 distinct geographical regions of India from **Grade 1 (Pandya Nadu South)** to **Grade 12 (Devabhumi Himalayas & Mount Kailash Graduation)**, teaching the 7 Sacred Ashram Books (*Narcheyal, Narpanbu, Nanneri, Nallaram, Narthunai, Narchol, Narchinthanai*) and *Thirukkural* wisdom.
3. **18 Famous Murugan Hill Temples & Shiva Temples (WGT Image-Driven Terrain & Sanctums)**:
   - **Murugan Hill & Seashore Temples**: *Thirupparamkunram, Tiruchendur, Palani (690 steps), Swamimalai (60 Tamil Year steps), Tiruttani (365 steps), Pazhamudircholai, Marudhamalai (837 steps), Kataragama, Kukke Subramanya*.
   - **Shiva Pancha Bhoota, Jyotirlinga & Himalayan Temples**: *Chidambaram, Tiruvannamalai (Arunachala), Rameswaram, Srisailam, Somnath, Ujjain Mahakaleshwar, Kashi Vishwanath, Kedarnath, Amarnath, Mount Kailash*.
   - Built with real uploaded 8K photographs and seamless photographic textures (`IndiaReliefMap`, `TempleStepsTex`, `HighwayRoadTex`, `HimalayaKedarnath`, and authentic temple exteriors & Garbhagriha sanctums).
4. **Multi-Modal All-India Transport**:
   - Switch between **`[1] 🦶 Walk (Padayatra)`**, **`[2] 🏍️ Bike`**, **`[3] 🚗 Highway Car`**, **`[4] 🚆 Vande Bharat Train`**, and **`[5] ✈️ Pushpaka Flight`**.
5. **100% Uncut Devotional Soundtrack at 50% Volume (`0.50`)**:
   - Plays 11 full-length uncut hymns from `@gurukuladesam` & `@shishyan` at `50%` volume without ever cutting off a song mid-way.
